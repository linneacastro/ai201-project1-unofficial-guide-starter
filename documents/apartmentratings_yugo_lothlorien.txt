Reviewer: Current Resident 472749
Rating: 4.8/5
Review: As a graduate student at UW, the location of the Lothlorien is excellent — a pleasant 15-minute walk to most classes. Being on the Ave, everything is easily accessible: Safeway and Trader Joe's a couple blocks away, tons of restaurants, coffee shops, and a cinema. The management and staff are extremely friendly and helpful. I love my apartment — very spacious with big windows, a balcony, and a lot of natural light. In-unit washer and dryer is a great time saver, and the underground parking keeps my car safe. Downsides: thin walls mean I can hear neighbors talking and the upstairs neighbor has a very heavy step. There are also homeless individuals loitering along the Ave, which can be problematic, though this is a general Seattle issue. Overall, of all the options nearby in a similar price range, I am really happy I chose this one.

Reviewer: StephyHong
Rating: 4.7/5
Review: I lived here for almost two years and would say this apartment is very suitable for students. Advantages: packages are safe and well managed by the leasing office; maintenance issues are fixed quickly; pricing is reasonable; advance notice is given about any construction. Disadvantages: no gym or study room; sound insulation is not great; residents need to go to the neighboring Wilsonian building to pick up packages. Despite some issues, the leasing office and managers clearly try to make the community better. I think the price is the best in the UW area for the facilities offered, and I would recommend it to friends.
